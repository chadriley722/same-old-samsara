who's behind this

chad + daniel.

We're Chad and Daniel, a couple who'd never homesteaded a day in our lives. In September 2026 we bought 34 acres in Central New York: meadow, woods and a river. We're building a homestead there, one tree at a time, with a food forest, a garden and more plants than we can name. We joke that it's our compound. And we're filming all of it. Summers on the land, winters on the road in our RV as Pneuma Nomads.

Same Old Samsara is Chad's creative outlet. It started with memes, then beads and patterns, and now it's mostly film. Later maybe screen prints, or things made from what grows on the land.

Samsara is the wheel: birth, death, coming back around. The raven sits on the ouroboros, the snake eating its tail. Seasons come back. Projects get set down and picked back up. The same old cycle.

what's here

  • Films: the documentary, a season a year on YouTube.
  • Wear: beaded earrings and bracelets made by hand, and apparel we designed, sold through our Etsy shop.
  • Beading patterns: brick stitch and bead loom PDFs. A few are free, the rest are in Samsara Digitals, our pattern shop on Etsy.
  • The bead lab: a free tool to design your own brick stitch pattern.
  • The field guide: the animals, plants and mushrooms we've found on the land so far.
