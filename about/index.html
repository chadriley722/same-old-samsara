about

who's behind this

chad + daniel.

same old samsara is chad's creative outlet. it started with memes, then beads and patterns, and now it's mostly film. later maybe screen prints, or things made from what grows on the land.

samsara is the wheel: birth, death, coming back around. the raven sits on the ouroboros, the snake eating its tail. seasons come back. projects get set down and picked back up. the same old cycle.

in september 2026 we (chad and daniel) bought 34 acres in central new york: meadow, woods and a creek. we're turning it into a small farm, one bed at a time, and filming all of it. summers on the land, winters on the road in our rv as pneuma nomads.

what's here

  • films: the documentary, a season a year on youtube.
  • beading patterns: brick stitch and bead loom pdfs on samsara digitals. a few free.
  • handmade jewelry: beaded earrings, bracelets and necklaces on etsy.
  • the bead lab: a free tool to design your own brick stitch.
