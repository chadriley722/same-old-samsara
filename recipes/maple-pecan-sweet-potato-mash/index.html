maple pecan sweet potato mash

Creamy sweet potatoes baked under toasted pecans and a drizzle of maple syrup.

6servings20min prep40min cook

I made this for a friend and they asked for the recipe. There wasn't one. I just went by feel, so this is my best guess at what I did. Taste as you go and make it yours.

ingredients

tap to check off as you go

steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and butter an 8x8 or 9x9 baking dish.
  2. Boil the sweet potatoes in salted water until fork tender, about 15 minutes.
  3. Drain well and let them steam dry in the pot for a couple of minutes, so the mash isn't watery.
  4. Mash with the butter, maple syrup, cream, cinnamon, nutmeg and salt. Taste and adjust.
  5. Spread it into the dish. Scatter the pecans on top and drizzle with the rest of the maple syrup.
  6. Bake about 25 minutes, until it's hot and the pecans are toasted.
notes

Make ahead: put it together a day early, keep it in the fridge, and add 10 minutes to the bake.

No nutmeg? 1½ tsp pumpkin pie spice works in place of both spices.
